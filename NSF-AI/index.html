About / Disclaimer

Purpose, limitations, and safety notes.

What this is

This dashboard is a static, client-side market intelligence terminal that combines technical analysis, market data, and news sentiment into decision-support signals for stocks and crypto.

What this is not

It is not guaranteed financial advice. Predictions are probabilistic and can be wrong. The app cannot promise profit and should not replace independent research, risk management, or professional advice.

How the scoring works

The engine blends trend, momentum, RSI, MACD, support/resistance, volume, volatility, sentiment, news impact, liquidity, and data freshness. Conflicting or stale inputs lower confidence.

Static hosting notes

The app runs from plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and JSON. Public endpoints may be added in Settings, but the dashboard still works with local JSON files and browser storage.
