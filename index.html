The conflict between the United States, Israel, and Iran has entered its 12th day, with tensions continuing to escalate across the Middle East. The war began on February 28, when the United States and Israel launched coordinated strikes on Iranian targets, claiming the operations were necessary to eliminate what US President Donald Trump described as “imminent threats” from Iran. Iran strongly denied accusations that it was pursuing nuclear weapons and condemned the attacks as a violation of its sovereignty. During the initial strikes, Iran’s long-time Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei and several senior military commanders were reportedly killed. Following his death, his son Mojtaba Khamenei was announced as the new leader, with officials saying he remains safe despite reportedly suffering injuries during the conflict. Iran has responded with a wave of retaliatory attacks targeting Israel and several Gulf countries. Overnight, Iranian bombing raids triggered panic in parts of Israel, where the Israeli ambulance service reported that 29 Israelis were injured in a stampede while people rushed toward shelters during the strikes. Israeli authorities say at least 11 civilians have been killed since the Iranian attacks began. The conflict has also spread to other parts of the region. According to the Saudi Ministry of Defense, Saudi air defense systems successfully intercepted and destroyed three ballistic missiles that were launched toward Prince Sultan Air Base. Meanwhile, the United States reported that 16 Iranian ships believed to be laying naval mines were “eliminated”, as Iran expanded its attacks across the region, raising fears of disruption to shipping lanes and broader regional instability. The United Arab Emirates has also been on high alert. Officials confirmed that the country destroyed eight ballistic missiles and intercepted 26 drones during the 11th day of Iranian attacks. Authorities reported that the death toll in the UAE has risen to six people, with 122 others injured so far as a result of the strikes and interceptions. On the diplomatic front, international leaders are increasingly concerned about the growing conflict. The French presidency announced that leaders of the Group of Seven (G7) will hold a video conference on Wednesday to discuss the war and possible steps to prevent further escalation in the Middle East. Meanwhile, Iran’s ambassador to the United Nations said on March 10 that more than 1,300 civilians in Iran have been killed as a result of the US-Israeli strikes, highlighting the severe human cost of the ongoing war.
