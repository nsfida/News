Who We Are

ISO-Certified Specialists in Waterproofing, Insulation & Protective Coatings

PRIME BUILD CONSTRUCTION CHEMICALS is an ISO 14001:2018, ISO 14001:2015, and ISO 9001:2015 certified company specialized in delivering high-end construction chemical solutions for the most demanding environments.

Our advanced application systems are engineered for durability, rapid curing, and long-term structural protection. We proudly serve industrial, commercial, and infrastructure projects across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
