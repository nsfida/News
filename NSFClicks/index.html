About NSF Clix

NSF Clix is the photography portfolio of Nadeem Shahzad Fida. The project focuses on documenting architecture, landscapes, culture and aerial perspectives across the Middle East and South Asia.

Through careful composition and storytelling, each photograph captures a moment of place, atmosphere and emotion.
