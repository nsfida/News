Secure Architecture Notice
This web portal is a VPN setup and control interface. A web page cannot directly create a system-level VPN tunnel — this requires a backend VPN service, OS-level native permissions, and platform-specific integrations (iOS Profile/NEVPNManager, Android VpnService API, macOS/Windows network drivers or a tunnel client). Pressing Connect calls our backend API at /api/vpn/connect, which allocates a server slot and returns configuration credentials. Your device then installs the provided VPN profile or configuration via the appropriate OS workflow. Credentials are ephemeral, encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3), and never logged.

Privacy Policy

We protect your data
by not collecting it.

Our privacy policy is simple: we collect the minimum required to operate the service, and nothing more.

01
No Activity Logs
We do not store websites visited, files downloaded, source IPs, DNS queries, or session timestamps.
02
Ephemeral Credentials
VPN credentials are generated per-session and automatically expire. They cannot be traced back to your account.
03
No Third-Party Sharing
Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or government entities without valid legal process.
04
RAM-Only Servers
All VPN servers run in RAM-only mode. No data survives a reboot. Physical seizure of hardware yields nothing.
05
Open Source Core
Our server-side tunnel software and client configuration generators are open-source and auditable on GitHub.
06
Independent Audit
Annual independent security audits verify our no-logs claims and server infrastructure integrity.
