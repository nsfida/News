The Khawrai Falahi Committee UAE was established in December 2016. The purpose of the committee is to address the issues faced by residents of Khawrai in the United Arab Emirates and its surroundings, and to resolve them in a timely and professional manner. The leadership of the committee is committed to serving the residents of Khawrai in the United Arab Emirates and is determined to address and resolve their various issues promptly.
