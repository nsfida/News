Architecture Note

This portal acts as a control interface. Activating the tunnel triggers a request to our backend API to provision a unique VPN profile. System-level encryption is managed by your OS native drivers once the profile is installed.

Setup FAQ

How to install on iOS?

Tap connect, allow the "Configuration Profile" download, and go to Settings > General > VPN to toggle.

Is it really free?

Yes, the NSF service is community-funded and supports zero-logging policies.
